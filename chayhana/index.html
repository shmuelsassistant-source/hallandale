The chaikhana

A tea house, the way Samarkand means it.

In Uzbekistan the chaikhana is where the neighborhood gathers: long tables, a pot of green tea, bread torn by hand, and a platter of plov in the middle. Chayhana Samarkand brings that table to Hallandale Beach, fully glatt kosher.

Regulars on Google call it the best plov and shashlik they have had in the country. Come hungry, come with people.

The table

From the mangal, the dough and the kazan.

A few of the dishes guests photograph most. The full menu and today’s specials are at the restaurant — call ahead for large parties.

From the mangal

Lamb shashlik
Marinated lamb, grilled over charcoal, served with raw onion and sumac.
Chicken shashlik
Tender skewers with a light char, on a bed of fresh herbs.
Lula kebab
Hand-minced ground meat shaped on the skewer, smoky and juicy.
Grilled fish
Whole fish from the grill with lemon and greens.

From the dough

Manti
Steamed dumplings in thin dough, filled with seasoned meat, served with sour cream.
Samsa
Flaky baked pastries from the tandoor.
Non
Round Uzbek bread, stamped and baked to order.

From the kazan

Plov
The dish Samarkand is known for: rice, carrots, chickpeas and lamb cooked slowly in a kazan.
Morkovcha
Spiced Korean-Uzbek carrot salad, bright and crunchy.
