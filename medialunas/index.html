La panadería

A little corner of Buenos Aires, en Hallandale.

In Argentina the day starts with a café con leche and a medialuna, the soft, sweet Argentine croissant. Medialuna’s brings that ritual to Hallandale Plaza: a bright, airy room, a case full of pastries and empanadas, and owners who greet you at the door.

Guests on Google rave about the size and freshness of the croissants, the savory dough of the empanadas and the warmth of a place run by its owners. Grab a coffee and sit, or take a box to the office.
