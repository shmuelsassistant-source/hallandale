How it works

No menu.
Just the counter.

  1. 01

    Walk in

    Everything is already cooked and warm, so there’s no waiting on the kitchen.

  2. 02

    Point & pick

    Choose a main from what’s out that day, plus two sides. Some guests pile three dishes on one plate.

  3. 03

    Stay or go

    Grab a stool by the window, or take it home. Pickup and delivery are online too.
