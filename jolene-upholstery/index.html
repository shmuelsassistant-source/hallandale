The work

A good piece deserves a second life.

Jo-Lene of Hallandale is a working upholstery shop, not a showroom. Dining chairs, sofas, recliners, antique settees and patio cushions come in tired and leave looking like the day they were bought — often better, because this time you chose the fabric.

Customers on Google talk about the same things again and again: fast turnaround, careful fabric selection, and Harry, who picks up, delivers and shows up on time.
