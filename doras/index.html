The atelier

A good fit is a quiet luxury.

Dora and Ana work the way tailors always have: pins first, chalk second, then the machine. Bring in the suit that never sat right, the dress for Saturday, the jeans you love. You try it on at pickup and anything gets tweaked on the spot.

Customers trust them with sequins, embroidery, lace and the expensive pieces, and with the emergencies: rush and same‑day work are a recurring theme in their reviews.

Services

What we do.

Everyday fixes to once‑in‑a‑lifetime gowns. If it's not listed, ask.

  1. 01

    Hems & lengths

    Trousers, skirts, sleeves and dresses, shortened or let down with the original finish kept.

  2. 02

    Suits & jackets

    Taken in, let out, sleeves and waists adjusted so the shoulders sit and the line falls clean.

  3. 03

    Bridal & evening

    Gowns, bridesmaids and black‑tie pieces, including sequins, lace and embroidery. Fittings at pickup.

  4. 04

    Zippers & repairs

    Replaced zippers, seams, tears, linings and the small fixes that keep a favorite in rotation.

  5. 05

    Denim & everyday

    Jeans tapered or hemmed with the original stitch, waistbands and casual wear fitted to you.

  6. 06

    Custom & Kay Diseños

    Made‑to‑order pieces and design work under the Kay Diseños name. Bring a sketch or a photo.

In a rush? Say so when you call. Turnaround depends on the piece, and many customers mention next‑day and same‑day help.

How a fitting works

  1. 1

    Bring it in

    Walk in or call ahead. Bring the piece and, if it helps, the shoes you'll wear with it.

  2. 2

    Pin & chalk

    Try it on. Dora or Ana pins the fit with you in the mirror and tells you when it'll be ready.

  3. 3

    Try, tweak, take home

    You try it again at pickup. Anything that isn't right gets adjusted on the spot.
