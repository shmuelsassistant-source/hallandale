The shop

A barber chair and a salon chair, under one roof.

Nelly’s is a neighborhood shop in Dania Beach, women-owned and Latino-owned, where regulars come back every couple of weeks. It’s a barbershop and a salon at once: crisp cuts and clean lines for the guys, styling for the ladies, and patient hands for the kids.

What people keep writing about: attention to detail, a friendly welcome and a cut that leaves you “looking nice and clean.”
