The bakery

Like the corner pékség back home.

Tastes of Europe is a small Hungarian bakery tucked into a Hollywood strip mall. Bread, pastries and cakes come out of the oven every morning, the coffee is on at the counter, and the shelves carry the European pantry staples that are hard to find anywhere else.

“The bread is always fresh and delicious, reminds me of being back home in Europe.”— Satinder, Google review

Our breads contain no sugars, artificial colors, flavors or preservatives.— from the bakery’s window

Every Saturday · 10 AM–4 PM

Lángos Saturdays.

Hungary’s favorite street food, fried fresh to order: a golden, puffy round of dough, piled with sour cream and shredded cheese. It’s on the chalkboard every Saturday, and only on Saturday.

Pair it with a coffee from the counter and a pastry for later.
